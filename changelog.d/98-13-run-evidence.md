- Every `probe-group live` run keeps `<log>.actions.json`, a passing one and the save-reload's second launch too, and every launch keeps a `<log>.input.csv`: about once a second, whose window was in front (the game's, another, none, or no game process), the cursor position and the last-input tick (no screenshot, no window title). Each result carries a `desktop` summary, and `probe-group live` prints it: the game not in front while it ran means a person or a dialog had the desktop.
