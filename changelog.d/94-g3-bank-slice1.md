- `gene bank put|verify|rebuild|list` (94.G3 slice 1): a rebuildable, content-addressed gene bank under `<data root>/Genes` (docs/GENE_BANK_DESIGN.md). Manifests are stored by the hash of their bytes, with a record per manifest, source and build, and blobs that re-hash as their members. History is kept outside the ROOT, so a rebuild in place and one into an empty folder on another machine give the same ROOT. `rebuild` deletes nothing.
